Privacy Policy for Bridget

Last Updated: October 2025

Introduction

Welcome to Bridget! This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (for authentication)
  • Name, birthdate, city, and profession
  • Profile photos
  • Bio and activity preferences

Usage Information

  • Matches and swipes
  • Messages sent through the app
  • App usage data

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Connect you with other users based on shared interests
  • Enable messaging between matched users
  • Improve the app experience
  • Send important updates about the service

Information Sharing

  • Profile Visibility: Your name, age, city, and activity preferences are visible to other users while browsing. Photos are only visible after matching.
  • We Never Sell Your Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
  • Matched Users: When you match with someone, they can see your full profile including photos.

Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored securely using Supabase
  • We use industry-standard encryption
  • Profile photos are stored securely with access controls

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Delete your account and data
  • Update your information at any time
  • Control who can see your profile

Data Retention

  • Account data is retained while your account is active
  • You can delete your account at any time through the app
  • Deleted data is permanently removed from our systems

Children's Privacy

Bridget is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes through the app or via email.
